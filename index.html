✦ Card Genie

Which card should you use?

Card Genie tells you which of the cards you already carry earns the most on the thing you are about to buy.

  • Rates are read off each issuer's own terms page by a person, and dated. Pages are re-checked every day, and the ones that change are read again.
  • It runs entirely on your device. There is no server, no account required, and no card number is ever asked for.
  • Where an issuer's terms cannot be verified, the card carries no rates at all rather than a plausible guess.
