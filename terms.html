What this application is

Card Genie tells you which of the credit cards you already hold is likely to earn the most rewards on a purchase you describe. It does this by applying published earning rates, entered by hand from issuers’ own terms pages, to the merchant and amount you tell it.

It is a calculator with a catalog. It holds no money, moves no money, connects to no bank, and has no relationship of any kind with any card issuer. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any bank, issuer, network or rewards programme named in it.

It does not apply for cards on your behalf, does not earn a commission when you use one, and contains no referral links. The order in which cards are ranked is produced by the same calculation for everyone and is not for sale.

Card information, and the limits of its accuracy

Earning rates come from issuers’ own terms pages, read by a person and recorded with the date they were read. An automated process re-checks those pages daily and flags the ones that have changed so a person can read them again. A substantial number of cards in the catalog deliberately carry no rates at all, because we would rather show a visible gap than a plausible guess.

Even so, and this matters: issuers reprice without notice; terms differ by when you opened an account, which version of a product you hold, and where you live; a page can say something we read wrongly; some issuers publish terms we cannot read automatically at all. Rates can be wrong or out of date, and some certainly are at any given moment.

The card data is provided as-is. Verify anything that matters against your own cardholder agreement before relying on it. If the application is wrong about a rate, you are responsible for what you spent — we cannot be, we would not know, and we have no way to make you whole.

The application is a research tool for a decision you make. It is not a guarantee of any reward, and we have no ability to cause any issuer to pay one.
